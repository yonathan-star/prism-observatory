THE LAST OBSERVATORY / PRISM MACHINE
Original AI-assisted CAD review draft for Yonathan — 18 October 2026

THE COURSE
Set the manual selector before feeding one nominal 8 mm PRINTED marble.
A: tangential bowl entry -> funnel throat -> sloped receiver -> merge.
B: vertical hopper -> lightweight tipping cup -> deep sloped receiver -> merge.
Both: shared approach -> three staggered trays and two guarded drops -> front collector.
The celestial instrument and service gauges/conduits are fixed sculpture.
Carved hall niches, dressed-stone courses, a broken survey pillar, recessed
funnel runes and leg lancets give the machine its ruined Observatory setting.
These details are actual CAD geometry. Render colors are illustrative finishes,
not a supplied multi-material toolpath.

PRINT QUANTITIES
1 chassis; 1 funnel; 1 cascade; 1 approach-bridge; 1 collector-ramp;
1 selector; 1 cup; 1 ballast;
1 balance-endclip; 1 axle; 2 retainers; 1 marble.
Print the coupon first. ballast-light is an optional lighter carrier, not an
additional installed weight. Do not install both carriers together.

BASELINE AND SCALE
Units are millimetres. Print at 100% scale. Chassis: 144 x 144 mm foundation,
3 mm thick, approximately 149.8 mm total height. Allow bed/brim clearance.
The official guide says to print an 8 mm marble. This design uses that baseline.
Start with a solid PLA marble (100% infill) and a solid regular counterweight.
The ideal solid 8 mm PLA sphere is about 0.33 g; actual mass/roundness depend on
the print and material. Weigh the ball and moving pieces before balance tuning.
The 0.8 mm cup wall is intended for roughly two 0.4 mm extrusion lines. Check
your slicer actually preserves it. These are design starting points, not a
validated printer profile. No G-code, slicer project or physical print is supplied.

ORIENTATION AND SUPPORTS
STLs are individually placed with their minimum Z at or close to the bed.
Keep all channels and bowls open upward. Inspect the slicer preview for every
layer: no sealed channels, missing thin walls, unsupported islands or trapped
support. The chassis contains elevated rails and substantial scenery; temporary
supports will be needed, and removal access must be planned. Do not accept a
support strategy that traps material in the cup bore, funnel throat, windows,
selector bearing, collector or rail interiors.
The cup and selector also need carefully removable underside support around
their hubs/stems. The tall funnel legs need stable adhesion. Small printed pins
may be fragile; an alternative is a straight 18.8 mm length of nominal 1.75 mm
filament, after measuring it. Support usability, strength and print time have
not been verified in a slicer. Do not start a long print before those checks.

FIT COUPON
Orient the notched corner at front left. Hole centres, measured from that
corner's nominal rectangle origin:
Front row Y=9: X=10/25/40/55/70 -> diameters 1.8/1.95/2.1/2.25/2.4.
Rear row Y=21: X=10/25/40/55/70/85 -> diameters 3.2/3.4/3.6/5.0/5.2/5.4.
All holes pass through the 4 mm plate. Test the actual axle and 3 mm locating
pegs. The coupon samples diameters; it does not certify every mechanism fit.
The selector stem is 4.6 mm in a nominal 5.0 mm bore. The module pins are
3.0 mm in 3.4 mm bores. The collector ramp uses 2.0 mm pins
in 2.4 mm bores. Cup/mount bores are 1.95 mm for the nominal 1.75 mm axle.
Retainer bores are 1.7 mm and intentionally grip the axle. Do not force brittle
retainers or enlarge them without preserving retention. Tune on spare samples.

ASSEMBLY (FRONT IS THE COLLECTOR SIDE; X RIGHT, Y REAR, Z UP)
1. Remove supports and debris. Check the printed 8 mm marble with calipers.
   Smooth seams without making a flat or changing its nominal size. Test the
   coupon. Leave funnel, selector and BOTH service rails off the chassis.
2. Seat the cascade at origin X=72,Y=42,Z=3. The first catch faces left and
   third tray exits right. Begin 35 mm FORWARD of its final position and 68 mm
   ABOVE its seated height. Slide REARWARD 35 mm, then lower onto four pegs.
   The checked path clears the fixed branch return and front survey pillar.
3. Hold the collector ramp 6 mm ABOVE its final position and 20 mm to the
   RIGHT. Slide LEFT into alignment, then lower it over its two keyed supports.
   Its outlet seats in the collector mouth. Do not force or glue the joints.
4. With the funnel still OFF, lower the approach bridge vertically onto its
   three keyed pier seats. Its inlet meets the shared merge and its outlet
   faces the first cascade catch. Keep the inlet/outlet gaps clear.
5. Lower the funnel at centre X=38,Y=104, feet on Z=3. Match its three keyed
   base pegs and tangential branch-A mouth. Then put the selector stem into
   the bearing at X=75.5,Y=116. Rotate manually to 225 degrees for A or 135
   degrees for B. Set it before feeding; the branch interface gap is 0.3 mm.
6. Set the cup between its bearing posts: axle centre approximately
   X=115.411,Y=104,Z=114. Bowl points forward, counterweight fork rearward.
   Thread the axle through both posts and cup. Fit one retainer outside each
   post. Confirm free rotation, positive axle capture and no rubbing.
7. Slide the regular counterweight into the rear fork so its neck and flange
   straddle the lever. Fit the endclip. Begin with the weight centre 9.7 mm
   behind the axle; reviewed range 9.5–10.5 mm. Secure a loose clip or sliding
   weight with a small removable tape tab outside moving/contact surfaces.
   Weigh the parts and tune physically. A lighter printed ball may need the
   optional light carrier or manual reset. Do not install both weights.

REMOVAL / MAINTENANCE (REVERSE OF THE CHECKED INSTALLATION PATH)
Remove selector; lift funnel vertically clear; lift approach bridge clear;
lift collector ramp 6 mm and slide it RIGHT 20 mm, then lift it clear;
lift cascade 68 mm, slide it FORWARD 35 mm, then lift clear. Do not lift the
cascade straight through the branch-return structure, or lift the approach
bridge through the funnel. Install in the reverse order above. Do not glue
service joints. Digital paths are sampled through full extraction above the
assembly, in 1–2 mm steps (ramp lowering: 0.25 mm). Physical hand/tool access,
fit, support removal and assembly forces still require dry-fitting.

CHECK AND OPERATE
The nominal cup stops are -5 degrees at rest and +55 degrees dumped. The
gravity-only estimate is supplied in the mechanical report. It ignores axle
friction, varying print mass, impacts and wear; it is not proof of triggering
or automatic empty reset. Never describe this draft as a tested working run.
On a level surface, try each stage with ONE ball, slowly and separately.
Check: selection and both gaps; funnel circulation and throat exit; cup loading,
tipping, release into the deep receiver and empty return; both merge approaches;
each guarded drop; final feed and collector. Record actual failures and tune.
Keep fingers clear of the cup and feed only after resetting the selector/cup.
If a ball stalls or escapes, stop feeding and correct the geometry/finish.
The static probe paths show available geometric envelopes; actual trajectories,
bounce and reliable retention still require a physical run.
